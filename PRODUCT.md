# Product

## Register

academic conference poster

## Users

Korean statistics conference reviewers, researchers, and attendees reading a
printed A0 portrait poster at varying distances. They need the research claim,
experimental setup, tables, and figures to be legible quickly while still
supporting closer technical inspection.

## Product Purpose

This workspace produces conference poster and presentation artifacts from
research context. The primary current surface is a one-page A0 portrait TeX/PDF
poster communicating that test accuracy alone is insufficient because optimizer
choice can alter calibration, feature geometry, and post-hoc OOD reliability.

## Current Poster Surface

- Title: `Optimizer-Induced Feature Geometry Shapes Post-Hoc OOD Detection Reliability`.
- Header: no subtitle; authors and affiliation appear as
  `GunHak Jin ∙ HyeYoung Jung Department of Mathematical Data Science, Hanyang University`.
- Key question: `비슷한 Accuracy라도 optimizer가 만든 feature geometry가 모델의 신뢰성에 어떤 영향을 미치는가?`
- Figure structure: one concept figure plus two empirical figures.
  Figure 1 explains miscalibration and OOD acceptance; Figure 2 shows the
  accuracy-matched reliability split; Figure 3 shows raw-to-L2 recovery.

## Scholarly Voice

This is not a commercial brand surface. It should read as a careful statistical
argument: evidence-led, precise, and restrained. The poster may be visually
confident at distance, but the authority should come from clear hierarchy,
tables, figures, and explicitly bounded claims rather than brand expression.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing hero layouts, decorative gradients, floating blobs, nested
cards, rounded card-heavy compositions, ornamental visual effects, cramped
paragraphs, and palettes that drift away from the established poster blues.
Do not clone the previous PPTX exactly; preserve its academic structure while
improving hierarchy and production quality.

## Design Principles

1. Distance-first hierarchy: title, section bars, tables, and figures must read
   before paragraphs.
2. Evidence over decoration: the composition should prioritize two empirical
   tables, one concept figure, and two empirical figures, not ornamental panels.
3. One-page discipline: every block earns its space on the A0 portrait page.
4. Token continuity: TeX changes should keep the design harness reusable for
   future HTML and PPTX work.
5. Academic restraint: use strong alignment, spacing, and type hierarchy rather
   than visual novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use high contrast black text on white, strong section labels, generous line
spacing, and large enough type for printed poster reading. Do not rely on color
alone in tables or figures; captions and labels should remain explicit.

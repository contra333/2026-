Interpretation

Feature-based OOD detectors rely on geometry. Optimizer and regularization choices can shift covariance scale, norm dispersion, and class compactness even when classification accuracy remains comparable.

Takeaways

Accuracy

Necessary but not sufficient for reliability.

Geometry

Explains detector-specific shifts.

Evaluation

Use paired deltas and geometry-OOD correlations.
